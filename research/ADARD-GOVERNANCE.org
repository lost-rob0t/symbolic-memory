#+title: ADARD Research Governance
#+status: ACTIVE
#+repo: lost-rob0t/symbolic-memory

* Purpose

ADARD/ARARD research in this repository is research-only and human-gated.
The loop may discover, compare, test, critique, and revise research documents.
It may not approve research for design or implementation.

* Hard rules

- Every research pass MUST produce or update a durable document under =research/=.
- Every research document MUST be returned to the human operator after the pass.
- Research status MUST remain human-gated until the operator explicitly changes it.
- ADARD/ARARD MUST NOT mark a document implementation-ready, design-ready, approved, accepted, or equivalent.
- ADARD/ARARD MUST NOT silently promote research because confidence is high or tests pass.
- The model MAY recommend a next state, but the recommendation is advisory only.
- The model MAY identify missing evidence and schedule another research pass.
- The model MAY revise an existing research document while preserving provenance and prior conclusions where useful.
- Implementation workers MUST consume only documents explicitly approved by the human operator.

* Required status vocabulary

Research documents begin as one of:

- =RESEARCHING=
- =PENDING-HUMAN-REVIEW=
- =NEEDS-MORE-RESEARCH=

Only the human operator may set:

- =APPROVED-FOR-DESIGN=
- =APPROVED-FOR-IMPLEMENTATION=
- =REJECTED=

* Required handoff

At the end of every pass, ADARD/ARARD MUST return:

1. the paths of all research documents created or changed;
2. the current status of each document;
3. a short statement of what changed;
4. unresolved questions or evidence gaps;
5. an explicit note that no approval or implementation promotion occurred.

The document itself is the handoff artifact. A prose summary alone is not sufficient.

* Implementation gate

An implementation worker may consume a research document only when an explicit human-controlled status permits it.

#+begin_src prolog
human_gate(researching, blocked).
human_gate('PENDING-HUMAN-REVIEW', blocked).
human_gate('NEEDS-MORE-RESEARCH', blocked).
human_gate('APPROVED-FOR-DESIGN', design_only).
human_gate('APPROVED-FOR-IMPLEMENTATION', implementation_allowed).
human_gate(rejected, blocked).

implementation_allowed(Status) :-
    human_gate(Status, implementation_allowed).
#+end_src

* Non-goals

- Autonomous research approval.
- Autonomous transition from research to implementation.
- Treating a high-confidence model judgment as equivalent to human approval.
- Returning only chat summaries without the underlying research artifact.
